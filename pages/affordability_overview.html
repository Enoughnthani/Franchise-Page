What Happens Next?
  • Open the selected record and complete the approved inputs.
  • Save and recalculate — calculated outputs cannot be manually overwritten.
  • If the record qualifies, choose Buyer's Certificate or Proceed to Submit to Bank.
Calculation Safeguards
  • Changing financial information makes the current result and any unissued output out of date.
  • Joint deals use one approved combined calculation.
  • Every calculation retains its rule version, user, date and history.
